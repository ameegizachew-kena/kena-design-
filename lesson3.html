🖌️ Logo jechuun maal?

Logo jechuun mallattoo, fakkii ykn barreeffama brand tokko bakka bu'uudha. Namni logo ilaaluun maqaa hojii ykn daldalaa sana yaadachuu danda'a.

  • 🎨 Design Company Logo
  • 📷 Photography Studio Logo
  • ▶️ YouTube Channel Logo
  • 🏢 Business Logo
  • 📱 App Logo
Logo gaariin:
✅ Salphaa ta'uu qaba.
✅ Dubbifamuu qaba.
✅ Yaadatamuu qaba.
✅ Hojii brand kee ibsuu qaba.

🎨 Color Sirrii Filachuu

  • 🟢 Green: guddina fi uumama.
  • 🔵 Blue: amanamummaa fi tasgabbii.
  • 🔴 Red: humna fi xiyyeeffannoo.
  • ⚫ Black: cimina fi professional.
  • ⚪ White: qulqullina fi salphina.
Logo tokko keessatti halluu 2 ykn 3 filachuun jalqabaaf gaarii dha.

🔤 Font Filachuu

Font jechuun bifa qubee ykn barreeffamaa ti.

  • Font dubbifamuu danda'u filadhu.
  • Font baay'ee walitti hin makiin.
  • Font maqaa brand waliin wal simsiisi.
  • Font salphaa yeroo baay'ee caala.
Modern: Brand haaraa fi technology.
Bold: Humna fi jabina.
Simple: Salphina fi qulqullina.

✨ Shadow, Stroke fi Effect

🌑 Shadow

Shadow jechuun gaaddisa text ykn object duuba jiru.

⭕ Stroke

Stroke jechuun daangaa text naannoo jiru.

💡 Opacity

Opacity jechuun wanti tokko hammam ifa ykn dukkanaa'aa akka mul'atu to'achuu dha.

Effect baay'ee hin dabalin. Logo salphaa fi qulqulluun yeroo baay'ee caala.
