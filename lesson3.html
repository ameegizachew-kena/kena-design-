🖌️ Logo jechuun maal?

Logo jechuun mallattoo, fakkii ykn barreeffama brand tokko bakka bu'uudha. Namni logo ilaaluun hojii ykn daldala sana salphaatti yaadachuu danda'a.

  • 🎨 Design Company Logo
  • 📷 Photography Studio Logo
  • ▶️ YouTube Channel Logo
  • 🏢 Business Logo
  • 📱 App Logo

✅ Hojii brand kee ibsa.

🎨 Color Sirrii Filachuu

  • 🟢 Green: guddina fi abdii.
  • 🔵 Blue: amanamummaa fi tasgabbii.
  • 🔴 Red: humna fi xiyyeeffannoo.
  • ⚫ Black: cimina fi professional.
  • ⚪ White: qulqullina fi salphina.
Logo tokko keessatti halluu lama ykn sadii fayyadamuun jalqabaaf gaarii dha.

🔤 Font Filachuu

Font jechuun bifa qubee barreeffamaa ti.

  • Font dubbifamuu danda'u filadhu.
  • Font baay'ee walitti hin makiin.
  • Maqaa brand waliin wal simsiisi.
  • Font salphaa filachuun gaarii dha.

✨ Shadow, Stroke fi Effect

🌑 Shadow

Shadow jechuun gaaddisa text ykn object duuba jiruudha.

⭕ Stroke

Stroke jechuun daangaa naannoo text jiruudha.

💡 Opacity

Opacity jechuun wanti tokko hammam ifa ykn dukkanaa'aa akka mul'atu to'achuudha.

Effect baay'ee hin dabalin. Logo salphaa fi qulqulluun yeroo baay'ee caala.
